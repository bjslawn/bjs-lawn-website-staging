Small Tree Removal & Trimming in Palatine, IL

Small and ornamental trees need occasional care to keep their shape, stay healthy, and stay clear of your home, walkways, and driveway. BJ's Lawn Service trims small trees to improve structure and clearance, and removes small trees that are dead, damaged, or planted in the wrong spot.

Whether a tree is crowding your house, hanging over a walkway, or simply no longer fits your landscape, we'll take care of it and clean up afterward. Removed trees can be followed up with stump grinding, new plantings, or lawn restoration.

Our Tree Trimming & Removal Process

1

Tree Evaluation

We look at each tree's size, condition, and surroundings and talk through whether trimming or removal makes the most sense.

  • Tree size and condition
  • Nearby structures and plantings
  • Trimming or removal recommendation
2

Trimming or Removal

We make clean cuts to shape and clear the tree, or take it down in sections with care around your home and landscape.

  • Clean, deliberate cuts
  • Care around structures and beds
  • Small tree takedown when needed
3

Cleanup

We clean up the branches and debris so your yard is left neat when we're done.

  • Branch and debris cleanup
  • Lawn and beds raked clean
  • Hard surfaces blown off
4

Follow-Up Options

After a removal, we can grind the stump and restore the area with topsoil, seed, sod, or a new planting.

  • Stump grinding
  • Lawn or bed restoration
  • Replacement planting
