Mulch Installation Services in Palatine, IL

Fresh mulch is one of the quickest ways to make a landscape look clean and finished. BJ's Lawn Service edges planting beds and installs mulch around foundation plantings, trees, shrubs, and flower beds for homes and businesses throughout Palatine and nearby suburbs.

Beyond looks, mulch helps planting beds hold moisture, keeps weeds down, and protects roots from summer heat and winter cold. We clean up the beds first and spread mulch evenly so your plants get the benefits without being smothered.

Our Mulch Installation Process

1

Bed Review

We look at your planting beds to estimate how much mulch is needed and talk through the color and type you'd like.

  • Bed measurements
  • Mulch type and color selection
  • Scheduling
2

Bed Cleanup & Edging

Before any mulch goes down, we clear leaves and debris, pull weeds, and cut clean edges along the lawn line.

  • Debris and leaf removal
  • Weed removal
  • Fresh bed edges
3

Mulch Installation

We spread mulch evenly through each bed, keeping it pulled back from plant stems and tree trunks.

  • Even coverage throughout beds
  • Mulch kept off stems and trunks
  • Clean transitions to lawn and walkways
4

Final Cleanup

We sweep and blow off walkways, driveways, and patios so the only thing left behind is a clean, finished landscape.

  • Hard surfaces cleaned off
  • Final touch-ups
  • Project walkthrough
