Shrub Removal Services in Palatine, IL

Shrubs that were planted years ago can outgrow their space, block windows and walkways, or decline from disease and winter damage. BJ's Lawn Service removes overgrown, dead, and unwanted shrubs so your landscape can get a fresh start.

We remove the shrub along with its root ball, clean up the debris, and leave the area ready for new plantings, a refreshed bed, or lawn. If you're planning what comes next, we can also handle the replacement planting, mulch, or sod.

Our Shrub Removal Process

1

Walkthrough

We walk the property with you to confirm which shrubs are coming out and talk through plans for the space afterward.

  • Shrubs identified for removal
  • Access and nearby plantings reviewed
  • Plans for the cleared area
2

Removal

We cut the shrub down and dig out the root ball, taking care around your home, walkways, and the plants you're keeping.

  • Shrub cut down
  • Root ball removal
  • Care around remaining plantings
3

Backfill & Grade

Holes are filled and the area is leveled so it's ready for new plantings, mulch, or lawn.

  • Holes backfilled with soil
  • Area leveled
  • Ready for replanting or sod
4

Cleanup

We haul away the branches, roots, and debris and clean up the work area.

  • Debris haul-away
  • Work area cleanup
  • Project walkthrough
