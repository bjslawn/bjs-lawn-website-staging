Stump Grinding Services in Palatine, IL

After a tree comes down, the stump left behind can be an eyesore, a tripping hazard, and an obstacle for mowing and landscaping. BJ's Lawn Service grinds stumps down below the surface so you can reclaim that part of your yard.

Once the stump is ground, we can clean up the grindings, fill the area with topsoil, and restore it with seed, sod, or new plantings so it blends back into your lawn or landscape.

Our Stump Grinding Process

1

Stump Evaluation

We look at the size and location of each stump, access to the area, and what you'd like to do with the space afterward. Contact JULIE to have underground utilities marked before grinding.

  • Stump size and location
  • Equipment access review
  • Underground utility locating
2

Grinding

We grind the stump down below the surface, along with any surface roots that would get in the way of mowing or new plantings.

  • Stump ground below grade
  • Surface roots addressed
  • Care around nearby beds and hardscapes
3

Cleanup & Fill

We clean up the grindings and fill the hole with topsoil so the area is level with the surrounding yard.

  • Grindings cleaned up
  • Topsoil fill
  • Leveled with surrounding grade
4

Restoration

If you'd like, we finish the area with seed, sod, mulch, or new plantings so it blends back into your landscape.

  • Seed or sod installation
  • Mulch or new plantings
  • Project walkthrough
