Sodding & Seeding Services in Palatine, IL

A thick, healthy lawn starts with good soil and the right installation. BJ's Lawn Service installs sod and seed to repair bare patches, replace worn-out or weedy turf, and establish new lawns after construction or landscaping projects.

Sod gives you an instant finished lawn, while seeding is a cost-effective way to fill in thin areas and establish grass over larger spaces. We'll help you decide which option fits your yard, timeline, and budget, then prepare the ground so your new grass can take root and thrive.

Our Sodding & Seeding Process

1

Lawn Evaluation

We look at the area, sun and shade, drainage, and the condition of the existing lawn to recommend sod, seed, or a combination.

  • Area size and measurements
  • Sun, shade, and drainage review
  • Sod or seed recommendation
2

Soil Preparation

Good prep is the difference between a lawn that takes and one that struggles. We clear the area and prepare the soil so new grass has a level, healthy base to root into.

  • Removal of dead turf and debris
  • Topsoil and grading as needed
  • Leveled, ready-to-plant surface
3

Installation

We lay sod with tight seams or spread seed evenly across the prepared area so the new lawn fills in uniformly.

  • Sod laid with tight, staggered seams
  • Even seed coverage
  • Clean edges along beds and walkways
4

Cleanup & Care Instructions

We clean up the work area and walk you through watering and care so your new lawn gets established.

  • Work area cleanup
  • Watering and mowing guidance
  • Project walkthrough
