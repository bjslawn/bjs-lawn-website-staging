How Our Services Fit Together

Most properties need a mix of work over the course of a year. Lawn & yard maintenance covers the recurring care: weekly lawn mowing starting at $45, spring and fall clean-ups, aeration and dethatching, weeding, weed control and fertilization, and gutter cleaning.

Landscaping services are one-time projects that repair or refresh part of the yard: new sod or seed, fresh mulch, removing shrubs, small trees, or stumps, and delivering topsoil, mulch, or gravel to your property.

Design & installation is where we plan and build. That includes hardscaping such as brick paver patios, walkways, and retaining walls, along with trees, evergreens, shrubs, and flower beds. Our crew has installed over 55,000 square feet of brick and 100,000 square feet of sod.
