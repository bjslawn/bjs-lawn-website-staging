Retaining Wall Design & Installation in Palatine, IL

A retaining wall holds back soil where the ground changes elevation, turning slopes and grade problems into level, usable space. BJ's Lawn Service installs retaining walls, seating walls, garden walls, and terraced landscapes that manage grading, control erosion, and add lasting structure to your property.

Whether you need to stabilize a slope, level out a backyard, frame a patio with a seating wall, or raise a planting bed, our team focuses on solid base preparation, proper drainage behind the wall, and materials that complement your home and existing landscape through every Midwest freeze-thaw cycle.

Our Retaining Wall Process

1

Planning & Layout

We review the slope, soil, drainage patterns, and how the finished wall should connect with your patio, planting beds, and yard. Contact JULIE to have underground utilities marked, finalize the contract, and apply for any required permits.

  • Site review, grades, and measurements
  • Wall height, location, and terracing plan
  • Block style and cap material selection
2

Excavation & Base Preparation

A retaining wall is only as strong as what it sits on. We excavate the wall line and build a compacted base so the first course starts level, solid, and ready to carry the wall above it.

  • Trench excavation along the wall line
  • Compacted gravel base installation
  • Level first-course setting
3

Wall Construction & Drainage

We build the wall course by course with the proper setback into the slope, then backfill behind it with drainage gravel so water moves away instead of pushing against the wall during wet seasons and freeze-thaw cycles.

  • Course-by-course block setting and alignment
  • Gravel backfill and drainage behind the wall
  • Reinforcement where wall height requires it
4

Finishing & Cleanup

Once the wall is built, we cap it, finish the grading, and restore the surrounding areas so the space is ready to plant, furnish, and use.

  • Cap installation and final detailing
  • Final grading, topsoil, and cleanup
  • Project walkthrough
