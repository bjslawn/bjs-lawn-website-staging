Landscape Planting & Flower Bed Installation in Palatine, IL

Planting is what brings a landscape design to life with color, texture, privacy, and seasonal interest. BJ's Lawn Service designs and installs flower beds, shrubs, trees, evergreens, and perennial plantings that fit your property, your maintenance comfort level, and the Midwest growing season.

The right plant in the right place makes all the difference. We select and position plants based on sunlight, soil, spacing, and long-term growth, so beds look full without becoming overgrown and new trees and shrubs have room to mature into the landscape.

Our Planting Process

1

Design & Plant Selection

We walk the property with you, look at sunlight, soil, drainage, and existing landscape, and build a plant palette suited to our Zone 5 climate and the look you want.

  • Site, sun, and soil review
  • Plant selection for climate and maintenance
  • Bed shape and layout planning
2

Bed Preparation

Healthy plantings start with healthy beds. We prepare each planting area so new plants establish quickly and beds stay clean and defined.

  • Bed edging and shaping
  • Weed and old growth removal
  • Soil preparation and amendments
3

Planting & Installation

We set out the full layout before anything goes in the ground, then plant at the proper depth and spacing so every tree, shrub, and perennial has room to grow into the design.

  • Layout and placement review
  • Proper planting depth and spacing
  • Initial watering-in of new plants
4

Mulching & Care Guidance

We finish the beds with mulch, clean up the work areas, and walk through simple watering and care guidelines so your new plantings establish well in their first seasons.

  • Mulch installation and final detailing
  • Site cleanup
  • Watering and care walkthrough
