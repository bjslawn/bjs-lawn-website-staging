Brick Paver Patio & Walkway Installation in Palatine, IL

Brick paving is a durable way to turn underused outdoor areas into practical, finished spaces. BJ's Lawn Service installs paver patios, walkways, borders, and hardscape features that fit the layout of your property and the way you use your yard.

From the first layout conversation to the final edging and cleanup, our team focuses on clear planning, solid base preparation, clean lines, and materials that complement your home, planting beds, and existing landscape.

Brick Paving Services

Paver Patios

Backyard paver patios for seating, grilling, outdoor dining, and everyday use.

Walkways

Entry paths and garden walkways that improve access and curb appeal.

Borders & Edging

Contrasting borders and detail work that frame patios, beds, and paths.

Outdoor Living Areas

Hardscape layouts that connect patios, planting, retaining walls, and yard features.

Our Brick Paving Process

1

Planning & Layout

We review the space, access points, drainage concerns, and how the finished patio or walkway should connect with your home and landscape.

  • Site review and measurements
  • Shape and traffic-flow planning
  • Material and border discussion
2

Base Preparation

A strong paver installation starts below the surface. We prepare the area for a stable, clean hardscape that can handle regular use.

  • Excavation and grading
  • Compacted base installation
  • Drainage-aware preparation
3

Paver Installation

We install the pavers, borders, and finishing details with attention to pattern, alignment, transitions, and overall fit with the property.

  • Paver setting and pattern alignment
  • Border and edge detail installation
  • Clean transitions to lawn and beds
4

Finishing & Cleanup

Once the hardscape is installed, we finish the surrounding areas and leave the space ready to use.

  • Joint finishing and final compaction
  • Edge cleanup and surface review
  • Project walkthrough
