Shrub Planting & Installation in Palatine, IL

Shrubs give a landscape its shape. They frame the house, fill the space between lawn and flower beds, and keep the yard looking finished in every season. BJ's Lawn Service designs and installs shrub beds that fit the scale of your property — full enough to look established, spaced so they don't become a pruning chore.

Whether you want flowering shrubs like hydrangea, lilac, spirea, and viburnum for seasonal color, or dense hedge and foundation plantings for structure and privacy, we choose varieties proven in Zone 5 and place each one where it has room to mature.

Our Shrub Planting Process

1

Design & Selection

We look at your beds, sunlight, and the look you want, then select shrub varieties that fit the space at maturity — mixing heights, textures, and bloom times where it makes sense.

  • Bed layout and sunlight review
  • Zone 5 hardy variety selection
  • Mature size and maintenance planning
2

Bed Preparation

New shrubs establish best in clean, healthy beds. We remove overgrown or declining plants, prepare the soil, and shape the bed edges before anything goes in the ground.

  • Old shrub and root removal
  • Soil preparation and amendments
  • Bed edging and shaping
3

Planting & Installation

We set the layout in the bed first, then plant each shrub at the proper depth and spacing so the bed looks full without shrubs crowding each other, the house, or walkways as they grow.

  • Layout and placement review
  • Proper planting depth and root care
  • Initial watering-in of new plantings
4

Finishing & Care Guidance

We finish the beds with mulch, clean up the work areas, and walk through watering and first-year care so your new shrubs establish quickly and stay healthy.

  • Mulch installation and final detailing
  • Site cleanup
  • Watering and pruning walkthrough
