Annual & Perennial Flower Bed Planting in Palatine, IL

Annuals and perennials are the fastest way to bring color, texture, and life to a landscape. BJ's Lawn Service designs and installs flower beds that brighten entrances, patios, walkways, and other high-visibility areas — combining annuals for bold seasonal color with perennials that return and fill in year after year.

We plan each bed around bloom times, sunlight, and spacing, so there is color through the growing season instead of one short flush in spring. Along the way, we favor plantings that hold up in Midwest summers and add pollinator value to your yard.

Our Flower Bed Planting Process

1

Design & Color Planning

We look at where color will have the most impact, how much sun each bed gets, and which combination of annuals and perennials fits your style and our Zone 5 growing season.

  • Sunlight and bed location review
  • Color palette and bloom-time planning
  • Annual and perennial selection
2

Bed Preparation

Flowers perform best in clean, healthy beds. We prepare each planting area so new annuals and perennials establish quickly and stay vigorous.

  • Weed and spent plant removal
  • Soil preparation and amendments
  • Bed edging and shaping
3

Planting & Installation

We lay out the design in the bed first, then plant at the proper spacing so annuals fill in quickly and perennials have room to mature without crowding.

  • Layout and placement review
  • Proper planting depth and spacing
  • Initial watering-in of new plantings
4

Finishing & Care Guidance

We finish the beds with mulch, clean up the work areas, and go over simple watering and deadheading tips so your flowers stay full and colorful.

  • Mulch installation and final detailing
  • Site cleanup
  • Watering and care walkthrough
