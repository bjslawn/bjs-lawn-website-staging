Tree Planting & Installation in Palatine, IL

A well-placed tree is one of the best long-term investments you can make in a landscape. It adds shade that cools your home and yard, privacy from neighbors and roads, and a focal point that grows more valuable every year. BJ's Lawn Service plants trees where they have room to mature — so the sapling you plant today becomes an asset, not a problem, decades from now.

We install shade trees like maple and oak, ornamental and flowering trees like crabapple, serviceberry, and redbud, and privacy trees along property lines — all chosen for Zone 5 hardiness and placed with mature height, root spread, and nearby structures in mind.

Our Tree Planting Process

1

Species & Site Selection

We start with what you want the tree to do — shade, privacy, spring color, or a focal point — then match species to your soil, sunlight, and available space.

  • Shade, privacy, and design goals review
  • Soil, sunlight, and drainage check
  • Zone 5 hardy species selection
2

Placement Planning

Trees are the longest-lived part of a landscape, so placement matters most. We position each tree for its mature canopy and roots, clear of foundations, driveways, utilities, and overhead lines.

  • Mature height and canopy layout
  • Setbacks from structures and utilities
  • Utility locate coordination before digging
3

Planting & Installation

We dig a properly sized hole, set the tree at the correct depth with the root flare exposed, backfill with amended soil, and stake only when the site calls for it.

  • Correct planting depth and root flare exposure
  • Soil amendments and backfill
  • Staking and deep initial watering as needed
4

Finishing & Care Guidance

We finish with a proper mulch ring — never piled against the trunk — clean up the site, and walk through watering and first-year care so your new tree establishes strong.

  • Mulch ring installation and final detailing
  • Site cleanup
  • Watering and first-year care walkthrough
