Landscape Site Planning & Layout in Palatine, IL

Every successful landscape starts with a good plan. BJ's Lawn Service helps homeowners and businesses decide where patios, walkways, planting beds, retaining walls, and trees should go so the finished project looks right, drains properly, and works with the way the property is actually used.

We look at the shape of your yard, sun and shade, slopes and low spots, entrances, and traffic patterns before anything is built. The result is a clear layout that ties each part of the project together and avoids costly changes once installation begins.

Our Site Planning Process

1

Consultation & Goals

We start by talking through how you want to use your outdoor space, what you like and don't like about it today, and the budget you have in mind.

  • Project goals and priorities
  • Style and material preferences
  • Budget and phasing options
2

Site Evaluation

We walk the property to review grades, drainage, sun exposure, existing plants, and structures so the plan is built around real site conditions.

  • Measurements and existing features
  • Slopes, low spots, and drainage patterns
  • Sun, shade, and soil conditions
3

Layout Plan

We put together a layout that places patios, walkways, walls, planting beds, and trees where they fit best and work together as one landscape.

  • Hardscape and planting bed placement
  • Walkway routes and access points
  • Material and plant recommendations
4

On-Site Marking & Installation

Before work begins, we contact JULIE to have underground utilities marked, then mark the layout on site so you can see exactly where everything will go before our crew starts installation.

  • Utility locating through JULIE
  • Layout marked and reviewed on site
  • Installation by our experienced crew
