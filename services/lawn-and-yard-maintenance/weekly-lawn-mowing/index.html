Weekly Lawn Mowing Services in Palatine, IL

A consistently mowed lawn is the foundation of a well-kept property. BJ's Lawn Service provides dependable weekly lawn mowing for homes and businesses throughout Palatine and nearby suburbs, with weekly service starting at $45.

Each visit includes mowing, trimming around obstacles, edging along hard surfaces, and blowing off clippings, so your lawn looks sharp every week without you having to lift a finger.
