Spring & Fall Clean-up Services in Palatine, IL

Midwest seasons are hard on a yard. BJ's Lawn Service offers spring and fall clean-ups that clear away leaves, sticks, and debris so your lawn and planting beds are ready for the season ahead.

A spring clean-up removes what winter left behind and gets your yard ready to grow. A fall clean-up clears leaves and cuts back plantings so your lawn and beds go into winter clean and healthy.

Fall Leaf Clean-ups
