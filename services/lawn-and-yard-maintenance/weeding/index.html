Weeding Services in Palatine, IL

Weeds compete with your plants for water, sunlight, and nutrients, and they can take over a planting bed quickly. BJ's Lawn Service pulls weeds from planting beds, borders, rock areas, and walkways so your landscape stays clean and your plants can thrive.

We remove weeds by the root whenever possible to slow regrowth, and we can pair weeding with fresh mulch to help keep new weeds from coming back.
