Lawn Weed Control & Fertilization in Palatine, IL

A thick, healthy lawn is the best defense against weeds, and the right feeding and weed control help you get there. BJ's Lawn Service provides lawn fertilization and weed control to help your grass grow greener and denser while keeping weeds from taking over.

We time treatments through the growing season so your lawn gets what it needs when it needs it. Combined with regular mowing and aeration, weed control and fertilization keep your lawn looking its best all year.

Weed Control & Fertilization Services

Lawn Fertilization

Feeding that supports thicker, greener grass through the growing season.

Pre-Emergent Weed Control

Early-season treatment to help stop weeds like crabgrass before they sprout.

Broadleaf Weed Control

Treatment for dandelions, clover, and other broadleaf weeds in the lawn.

Seasonal Timing

Treatments scheduled through the season for the best results.

Lawn Evaluation

  • Weed types identified
  • Treatment recommendation
